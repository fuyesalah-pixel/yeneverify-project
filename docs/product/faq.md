# Frequently Asked Questions

> Last updated: 2026-09-24 | Owner: Fuad

---

## General

**What is Yene Verify?**
A mobile app that verifies Ethiopian bank payment receipts in under 10 seconds.
Take a photo of the customer's confirmation screen — the app tells you if the
receipt is genuine, fake, or has been used before.

**Which banks are supported?**
CBE, Telebirr, Awash, Dashen, Bank of Abyssinia, M-Pesa, and Zemen Bank.

**How fast is a verification?**
Median under 10 seconds. Results are delivered via push notification so
you don't need to wait in the app.

**Does the app work without internet?**
No. Yene Verify requires an internet connection to verify receipts.
An offline mode is not planned for the current version.

---

## Receipts

**What types of photos work best?**
Photos taken directly of the customer's screen. Good lighting, minimal glare,
the full confirmation screen in frame. Blurry or cropped photos may fail OCR.

**Can I upload a screenshot from my gallery?**
Yes. The app supports both live camera capture and gallery upload.

**What if the result is "Needs Review"?**
The verification was ambiguous — not clearly genuine or fake. This goes to
a review queue. You'll receive a notification once it's resolved.

**What does "Duplicate" mean?**
The same receipt has already been verified — either by you or by another
merchant. This protects you from customers reusing the same payment proof.

## Billing

**How do I pay for a subscription?**
Transfer the package amount via CBE or Telebirr to the Yene Verify account
shown in the app. Then upload the payment screenshot. Credits are issued
automatically once verified.

**Can I get a refund?**
Contact support at support@yeneverify.com. Refund decisions are made manually.

**What if my payment verification failed?**
Check that you transferred the exact amount to the correct account. If the
issue persists, contact support with your transaction reference.

## Team (Sub-Merchants)

**Can I add employees to my account?**
Yes. Go to the Team section and add team members. They'll receive an SMS
invitation and can start verifying payments under your account.

**Can my employees see my revenue?**
No. Team members (sub-merchants) can only see verifications they personally
submitted. Full analytics and reports are visible only to you (the merchant).

**Is there a limit on team members?**
No limit. Add as many as you need.

---

## Privacy & Security

**Who can see my verification data?**
Only you and your team members (sub-merchants) can see your data.
Yene Verify staff can only access data for platform management purposes.

**Are payment receipts stored?**
Receipt images are stored securely in encrypted cloud storage and used only
for verification. They are not shared with third parties.

**What happens if I lose my phone?**
You can revoke your old device from a new phone by re-verifying your phone
number and removing the old device from Trusted Devices in settings.
