# Logo Brief

> Last updated: 2026-09-24 | Owner: Fuad

---

## Brand Name

**Yene Verify** — two words, both capitalized.
Never: "YeneVerify", "yeneVerify", "Yene verify".

In the wordmark: "Yene" in regular weight, "Verify" in semibold.

---

## Taglines

- Primary: *"Verify any Ethiopian bank payment. In seconds."*
- Short: *"Verify. Trust. Grow."*

---

## Mark Direction

The logo mark should feel like a **trust symbol** — not playful, not casual.

Options to explore:
1. A **shield** with a checkmark formed from a receipt edge in negative space
2. A stylized **"Y"** that doubles as a checkmark
3. A **receipt corner** with a verification tick overlay

---

## Colors

| Role | Hex |
|------|-----|
| Primary (background, filled mark) | `#16A34A` |
| Accent (checkmark stroke only) | `#39FF14` |
| Mono light | `#FFFFFF` |
| Mono dark | `#0A0A0A` |

⚠️ `#39FF14` (neon) must never be used as a fill behind white text.
Use it only as a stroke, thin line, or small accent.

---

## Typography

Wordmark font: geometric sans-serif, medium weight, tight letter-spacing.
Recommended: Inter, Geist, or a custom geometric sans.

---

## Sizes

| Asset | Minimum size |
|-------|-------------|
| Mark alone | 24px height |
| Wordmark | 120px width |
| App icon | 1024×1024px (store submission) |

---

## Clear Space

Minimum clear space around logo: 1× cap-height on all sides.
